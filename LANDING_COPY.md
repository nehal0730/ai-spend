## FAQ

### 1. Do I need integrations?

No. The current product starts with manual input so you can get a baseline without wiring up vendor accounts.

### 2. How are recommendations generated?

Recommendations come from deterministic rules that compare spend, seats, overlap, concentration, and plan fit.

### 3. Can I share the report?

Yes. Every completed audit can generate a public share page link.

### 4. What happens if the AI summary fails?

The summary falls back to deterministic copy built from the report values.

### 5. Is this a full procurement platform?

No. It is a focused audit tool for finding AI cost waste and deciding what to fix first.
